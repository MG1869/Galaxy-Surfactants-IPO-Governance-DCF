## Methodology & Key Findings

### 1. Corporate Governance
- **Ownership**: Not family-run in the classic sense — promoters hold ~70.9%, but management is professional.
- **Board**: 8 members (FY25), including 4 independent directors and 2 women directors.
- **No CEO duality**: Managing Director (K. Natarajan) and Chairman (independent, non-executive) are separate individuals — supporting stronger independent oversight.
- **Auditor**: Deloitte Haskins & Sells LLP issued an unmodified opinion, confirming reliable financial reporting and an intact audit-trail system.
- **Litigation**: Ongoing disputes include a ₹73.74 Cr land litigation (GIDC), plus smaller customs/excise/GST claims — none deemed a going-concern risk.
- **Conclusion**: Low likelihood of information asymmetry between board and management, given independent board composition and clean audit opinions.

### 2. IPO Analysis
| Metric | Value |
|---|---|
| IPO Date / Listing | Jan 29–31, 2018 / Feb 8, 2018 |
| Issue Price | ₹1,480 |
| Listing-Day Gain | **+14.9%** (underpriced) |
| Structure | 100% Offer for Sale (OFS) |
| Oversubscription | **~19.96×** overall (QIB 54.27×, NII 6.96×, Retail 5.92×) |
| Amount Mobilized | ₹937.09 Cr |
| Promoter Holding (pre → post) | 76.97% → 70.91% |
| Lead Underwriters | ICICI Securities, Edelweiss, JM Financial |

- Being a 100% OFS, the IPO gave existing shareholders (promoters/early investors) an exit route; the company itself received no proceeds.
- **Verdict**: The IPO was a success — strong oversubscription and positive listing-day gain reflected solid market confidence, and the company has sustained steady performance post-listing.

### 3. Financial Management

**Capital Expenditure**
- FY25 capex: ₹165.32 Cr (+17.6% YoY), just 3.9% of revenue — an asset-light profile.
- CWIP jumped 65.3%, signaling an active expansion pipeline (new capacity in Europe, revived presence in the Americas).
- Mix: ~60% expansion, 30% modernization, 10% replacement — funded entirely through operating cash flow (₹420.51 Cr), with no equity dilution or heavy new debt.

**Working Capital**
- Working capital grew from ₹850 Cr (FY24) to ₹993.56 Cr (FY25).
- Efficiency improved slightly: inventory days down to 65, receivable days down to 55.3, payable days down to 70 — indicating a marginally tighter cash conversion cycle.

**Capital Structure**
- Debt/Equity ratio has stayed remarkably stable and low, ~0.087–0.089 across all 8 quarters (Q1 FY24–Q4 FY25) — a conservative, largely equity-funded balance sheet.
- Capex requirements are comfortably met by internal operating cash flows rather than external borrowing.

**Cost of Capital & Intrinsic Value (DCF)**
- **Cost of Equity (CAPM)**: 11.7% (risk-free rate 6.5%, beta 0.8, ERP 6.5%)
- **After-tax Cost of Debt**: 7.35%
- **WACC**: 11.57% (market-value weighted)
- Valued using a **single-stage (Gordon Growth) DCF**, appropriate given the firm's 30+ year maturity and stable FY24–FY25 performance, assuming a 4% terminal growth rate in line with long-run nominal GDP growth.
- **Estimated intrinsic value: ~₹395/share** (Enterprise Value ₹1,395.7 Cr → Equity Value ₹1,401.5 Cr ÷ 35.5M shares).
